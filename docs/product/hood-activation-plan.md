# Hood Activation & Launch Challenge: Business and Engagement Plan

_Written 17 Nov 2026, the day the Launch Challenge went live on social media. Owners: Product (plan), Community Ops (content and moderators), Engineering (build). The content itself is in [hood-content-bank.md](hood-content-bank.md). The build plan is the engineering work packages at the end of this document._

---

## 1. The problem in one sentence

People who join myHoodora today often land in a Hood with no posts. An empty Hood tells them "nobody is here", so they don't come back. This happened to our own founder on sign-up.

The Launch Challenge is now sending people to the app, so every empty Hood is wasting the attention we are paying for.

**The goal:** every new neighbour's first visit passes the **five-second test**: *within five seconds of opening my Hood, does it feel lived-in and useful?* After that, they have a reason to come back tomorrow.

## 3. What "activated" means

A Hood is **activated** when all five are true:

1. **15 or more visible items** (posts, polls, events, listings) in the last 30 days.
2. **5 or more verified neighbours.**
3. **At least one Founding Neighbour**: a real resident who has agreed to post and welcome people.
4. **A pinned welcome post** from the Hood Team.
5. **A named staff moderator** responsible for it.

Admin → Insights → Activation shows every Hood as **green** (all five), **amber** (3–4) or **red** (0–2). We send traffic to green Hoods first. Red Hoods get extra seed content and a Founding Neighbour search before we promote them.

## 4. Who does what

| Role | Who | What they do | What they never do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hood Team** (official account) | One labelled account, "myHoodora Hood Team", run by Community Ops | Welcome posts, conversation starters, local information, daily prompts, weekly "new neighbours" note | Pretend to be a neighbour. Every post carries the **Official · Hood Team** label |
| **Staff moderators** | Our moderators, each responsible for a cluster of about 25 Hoods in launch weeks | Reply to newcomers within 2 h, keep conversations going, spot problems, recruit Founding Neighbours | Post as anyone else. They reply as themselves with a **Staff** label, or as the Hood Team |
| **Founding Neighbours / Hood Leads** | Real residents (existing Hood Leads, estate executives, early challenge sign-ups) | Post real local content, invite neighbours, welcome people, vote on reports (existing Lead role) | Need to be "on" all day. A few good posts a week is enough |
| **Community Ops lead** | One named person | Owns the content calendar, the moderator rota and the weekly readiness review | |

**Rota:** 07:00–22:00 WAT, two shifts. Launch weeks: about 1 moderator per 25 Hoods, which is 7–8 moderators for our ~180 Hoods. After the first month this drops to 1 per 50 as organic activity grows.

## 5. The Launch Challenge: how it works as a business tool

**What it is:** a social-media campaign (planned offline, live since **17 Nov 2026**) that brings Hood Leads, moderators and early neighbours on board to **build up their Hoods**, and raises awareness of the new app update.

**Who earns the Builder badge:** anyone who, during the challenge, does either of these:
- **invites 3 neighbours who go on to verify their address**, or
- is **active on 5 different days** (posting, replying, voting in a poll or RSVPing; reactions alone don't count).

Admins can change both numbers. Activity is counted back to 17 Nov, so early people aren't left out. Invites sent before tracking existed are credited through a one-off "Who invited you?" question.

**There is no fixed end date.** The challenge runs while it pays off. Every Monday the Ops lead reads the Challenge dashboard and makes one of three calls:

| Signal on the dashboard | Decision |
|---|---|
| Weekly verified sign-ups from invites and shares are **growing**, or above the target per active Hood | **Keep going**: same plan |
| Sign-ups are fine overall but some Hoods stay **red** | **Push**: more seed content, Founding Neighbour search and QR posters in those Hoods |
| Sign-ups from the challenge stay **below target for 2 weeks running**, or **80%+ of target Hoods are green** | **End or rebrand**: admin presses "End challenge". Badges already earned stay |

_Targets to set in week 1 from the first numbers: verified sign-ups per active Hood per week, and the share→sign-up conversion rate._

## 7. A Hood's first 10 days (the activation plan)

Seed content is **spread out**, not dumped in at once, so the Hood looks naturally active. Each Hood gets this sequence, with `{hood}` and `{city}` filled in:

| Day | Morning (08:00) | Evening (18:00) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 📌 **Welcome post** (pinned 7 days) | Local info: emergency numbers |
| 1 | Starter: "Introduce yourself" | Poll: biggest issue on our street |
| 2 | Local info: power company and fault line | Starter: best mechanic / tailor / plumber |
| 3 | Daily prompt (weekday theme) | Poll: favourite local spot |
| 4 | Local info: waste collection | Starter: lost & found |
| 5 | Daily prompt | Starter: weekend plans / events |
| 6 | Declutter Saturday (marketplace) | Thank a neighbour |
| 7 | Weekly "new neighbours joined" note | Daily prompt |
| 8–9 | Daily prompts take over | |

After day 9, the **daily prompt** is the only scheduled Hood Team post. Once a Hood has **50+ weekly active neighbours**, the Hood Team posts at most **3 times a week**.

### Weekly rhythm of daily prompts

| Day | Theme | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Power and utilities | "How has light been on your street this week?" |
| Tuesday | Trusted people | "Who's the most reliable electrician you've used around {hood}?" |
| Wednesday | Poll | "Best suya spot near {hood}?" |
| Thursday | Safety | "One safety habit everyone on our street should know?" |
| Friday | Weekend | "Anything happening around {hood} this weekend?" |
| Saturday | Declutter | "Got something you no longer need? List it free for a neighbour." |
| Sunday | Gratitude | "Shout out a neighbour who helped you this week 🙏" |

## 8. What people see when a post is shared

Posts have an **audience**, chosen when writing. This is the same model as Nextdoor:

- **Your Hood** (default): only your Hood reads it in full.
- **Nearby Hoods**: your Hood and the Hoods next to it.
- **Anyone**: anyone with the link, even without an account.

| Someone opens a shared link… | What they see |
|---|---|
| Post shared to **Anyone** (Hood Team posts, listings and events are Anyone by default) | The full post and replies, with **initials only** for names. Joining is needed to react or reply |
| **Your Hood** or **Nearby** post, viewer outside that audience | A **teaser**: which Hood, the topic, how many replies. No words, photos or names |
| Safety alert | A teaser only, never the location |
| Removed, deleted or private-group post | "This post is no longer available" and a link to explore their own area |

**After signing up, you join your own Hood, not the one in the link.** If a Yaba resident opens a Lekki post and signs up, they land in **Yaba** (already seeded), with a small card: "You came from a Lekki post". The card opens the post in full only if its audience allows it. The person who shared the link still gets invite credit once the new neighbour verifies.

**When the person has the app:**
- **Today:** links open in the browser. The page has an **"Open in the app"** button.
- **Next store build:** links open **straight in the app** on Android and iPhone. This uses Android App Links and iOS Universal Links, the system Facebook, YouTube and Nextdoor use.
- **Exception:** links tapped inside Instagram's or Facebook's own browser stay in that browser (those apps block it). The page's button covers that case.

**When they're on the website, we invite them to the app the way the big platforms do, without getting in the way:**
- **Phones:** a slim banner. It says "Open in the app" if they have it, or "Get the app" if they don't, and opens the same page in the app. On iPhone this is Apple's own Smart App Banner.
- **After signing up or logging in on a phone,** or on a second visit: a "myHoodora is better in the app" sheet with "Open the app" and "Continue in browser". Shown at most once a week.
- **Computers:** a "Get the app" QR code.
- **Never on first arrival.** Google lowers the search ranking of sites that cover content with app-install pop-ups.

**Why the teaser:** neighbours post about their homes, streets and safety. Under the NDPA and for basic trust, their words are never shown to the wider internet unless they chose *Anyone*.

## 9. Invites and referrals

Everyone gets a short **invite code** (e.g. `ADA7K`) and a link, `myhoodora.com/i/ADA7K`. These ways of joining credit the person who invited you:

1. Your personal invite link (WhatsApp, SMS, social bios).
2. **Any post, listing or event you share.** Shares count as invites.
3. Your "Hood Builder" badge share image.
4. Group invite links.
5. A printable **QR poster** for estate gates, churches, mosques and markets.
6. Typing the code at sign-up, for flyers and word of mouth.
7. A one-off **"Who invited you?"** question for anyone who joined since 17 Nov without a code.

**Fair-play rules:**
- The first code wins.
- You can't invite yourself.
- An invite **only counts once the new person verifies their address** and confirms they're 18+.
- Bursts of sign-ups from one device are flagged for review.
- Admins can correct credit, and every change is logged.
- People who invite from the **same Hood** get priority in the staff review queue. Their location check is never skipped.

## 10. Badges and streaks

- **The Builder badge** is the **myHoodora mascot** on the corner of your profile photo, like the check mark on X. Tapping it explains what it means: "Hood Builder · 12-day streak · Launch Challenge".
- **The streak ring** around the badge shows how consistently you show up:
  - **bronze** = 3-day streak
  - **silver** = 7 days
  - **gold** = 30 days
- **Kind by design.** Everyone gets **one free rest day a week**. A lost streak lowers the ring by one tier after a week rather than wiping it. There are no countdown timers. At most one gentle reminder at 18:00, only if you have a 3+ day streak, and you can turn it off.
- **Kept apart from "Verified neighbour".** The badge says *active*, never *verified identity*. The Verified check stays a separate symbol, so nobody confuses the two the way people did with X's blue check.

## 11. Christmas and New Year

| Season | Dates (WAT) | What people see |
|---|---|---|
| **Christmas** | 15 Dec 00:00 → 27 Dec 00:00 | Twinkling lights along the top of the app, an occasional sparkle, the mascot in a Santa hat, and a "Merry Christmas from {hood}" greeting once a day. **No snow**: it doesn't fit Nigeria |
| **New Year** | 31 Dec 18:00 → 2 Jan 00:00 | A **crossover countdown** on the 31st, a **fireworks** burst at midnight and on first open on 1 Jan, and the mascot in a party hat |

- **Admin control:** both are switched on and off by **start and end dates** in Admin → Settings → Platform → Seasons. Admins can change dates, effects and greeting text, or preview them on their own account.
- **Considerate:** effects play for about 8 seconds and then settle. They respect the phone's "reduce motion" setting, and anyone can turn them off in Settings → Appearance.
- **Delivery:** effects ship as an over-the-air app update, so no new store build is needed.

## 12. How we measure success

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| New users who see **10+ items** on their first visit | 100% |
| Hoods that are **green** (activated) | 50% by 1 Dec, 80% by 31 Dec |
| Median time to the **first reply** on a newcomer's post | Under 2 hours |
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention of new sign-ups | Baseline in week 1, then +20% |
| Weekly active neighbours per Hood | Grows every week of the challenge |
| Share opened → signed up → verified | Baseline in week 1, then improve |
| Share of posts by the Hood Team | **Below 30%** of all posts by 30 days after a Hood activates |
| Builders badged · neighbours on a 7+ day streak | Tracked weekly |

The last-but-one measure keeps us honest: the Hood Team is scaffolding. If its share isn't falling, real neighbours aren't taking over, and we fix that before adding more content.

## 13. Risks and guardrails

| Risk | Guardrail |
|---|---|
| Seeded content looks fake or misleading | Seed content only ever comes from the labelled **Hood Team** account. No fake neighbours, ever (App Store guideline 1.2, Google Play policy, and basic trust) |
| Hood Team posts break our own rules | The same content filter and guidelines apply to official posts |
| Privacy (NDPA) | New neighbours are never named in public posts. Neighbour posts are never shown outside their audience. Only initials appear to signed-out visitors |
| Invite farming | Only **verified** invitees count. Device bursts are flagged, and admins can void credit |
| Moderator burnout | Fixed rota and clusters of ≤25 Hoods. The "needs a reply" list shows what matters first |
| Streak pressure | Rest day, slow decay, no night pushes, opt-out |
| Battery and data use of festive effects | Short, light, respect reduce-motion, can be turned off |
| Too much Hood Team content | The Hood Team share target above, and fewer Hood Team posts once a Hood passes 50 weekly active neighbours |
