# Training log

The record an NDPC audit asks for. Part 1 covers our Data Protection Officer's certification and yearly professional development. Part 2 covers privacy training for everyone else. Each entry needs evidence, kept in the private compliance folder rather than the repo, because certificates carry personal details.

Source: NDPC Guidance Notice NDPC/HQ/GN/VOL.05/26, *Schedule 3: Data Protection Officer's Assessment* (GAID 2025), dated 26 June 2026 ([PDF](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Guidance-Notice-on-CPD-for-Data-Protection-Officers.pdf)). **Getting certified.** The NDPC runs its own DPO Training and Certification. The last cohort is closed; watch [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng) for the next one. Alternatively, take a recognised international privacy certification with an exam. Then enrol on the NDPC's DPO database.

### Yearly CPD rule

- At least **20 points a year, out of 40** available, to stay "active". **At least 10** must be Category A (structured training).
- Reviewed **every year at re-validation**. Falling short can mean "temporarily inactive" until it's made up.
- Evidence the NDPC accepts:
  - certificates of attendance;
  - confirmation emails;
  - copies of, or links to, publications;
  - formal letters of participation;
  - PEER forms (Professional Education and Engagement Review).

**A realistic first year (22 points, 13 of them Category A):**
- Virtual Privacy Academy course (4, A)
- one 4-hour NDPC-accredited programme (5, A)
- two IMPACT Series webinars (2 + 2, A)
- joining a privacy professional body (2, C)
- one privacy conference (3, C)
- running our staff briefing (Part 2) and writing it up as approved training material (3, B)
- answering one NDPC consultation (1 to 2, C), if one opens

## 2. Staff privacy training

Everyone who can see members' data (staff, moderators, Hood Leads with staff tools, contractors) takes a briefing **at least every 6 months**, and again when they join (GAID 2025 Art. 46(3); NDPC training guidance). It is run by the DPO or uses the Virtual Privacy Academy. It takes about one hour and covers:
- the NDPA principles and lawful bases;
- members' rights, and how a privacy request reaches the DPO (the "Privacy & data request" topic);
- what our security measures are ([../security.md](../security.md)) and what not to do with exported data;
- spotting and reporting a breach ([ndpa.md](./ndpa.md) §8: the 72-hour rule);
- children: under 18s are closed and deleted.
